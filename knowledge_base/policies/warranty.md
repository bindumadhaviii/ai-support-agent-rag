# Ridgeline Guarantee (Warranty)

Our gear is built to be used. The Ridgeline Guarantee covers **defects in materials
and workmanship** from the delivery date for:

| Product type | Coverage |
|---|---|
| Tents and backpacks | 2 years |
| Jackets and footwear (including boot waterproofing) | 1 year |
| Lanterns and electronics | 1 year |
| Socks and accessories | 1 year |

## What's covered

- Seams, zippers, buckles, or stitching that fail during normal use
- Boots that leak because of a membrane defect
- Tent poles that break without impact or misuse
- Lanterns that stop charging or holding a charge

## What's not covered

- Normal wear and tear, such as worn outsoles or faded fabric
- Damage from accidents, misuse, animals, fire, or improper care
- Items that were altered or repaired by someone else
- Lost parts

## How to make a claim

Contact support with:

1. Your order number
2. Photos showing the problem
3. A short description of what happened

We'll review your claim within 2 business days. Depending on the issue, we'll
**repair** the item, **replace** it, or issue **store credit** if it's no longer
available. Warranty claims don't require the item to be within the 30-day return
window.
