# Damaged, Defective, or Wrong Items

If your order arrives damaged, has a defect, or isn't what you ordered, we'll make
it right at no cost to you.

## Report it within 7 days

Contact support within **7 days of delivery** with:

- Your order number
- Photos of the item and, if possible, the packaging
- A short description of the problem

## What we'll do

You can choose a **replacement** or a **full refund**, including the original
shipping charge. Return shipping is always free for these cases.

For items under **$50**, we usually won't ask you to send the item back.

## After 7 days

Problems found after 7 days are handled under the Ridgeline Guarantee, which covers
manufacturing defects for 1 or 2 years depending on the product.

## Missing items

If a package arrives with an item missing, contact us within 7 days. Some orders
ship in more than one box, so check your tracking for a second package first.
