# Refund Policy

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to your **original payment method**. If you paid partly with a
gift card, that portion is returned to your gift card balance. We cannot refund to
a different card or account.

## Timeline

1. Your return is inspected within **2 business days** of reaching our warehouse.
2. Once approved, we issue the refund the same day and email you a confirmation.
3. Your bank or card provider usually shows the refund within **5–10 business days**.

For damaged, defective, or wrong items where we don't need the item back, the refund
is issued as soon as your claim is approved.

## Refund review for larger amounts

Refunds over **$200** are reviewed by a member of our support team before they are
issued. This adds up to **1 business day**. You'll get an email as soon as the
refund is approved.

## What is refunded

- The price you paid for the returned items, including tax
- **Not refunded:** original shipping charges, unless the item was damaged,
  defective, or we made an error
- A **$7.95 return shipping fee** is deducted unless it is waived
  (see *Return Policy*)

## Partial returns

If you return some items from an order, you're refunded only for those items. If
your order qualified for free shipping and the remaining items fall below $75,
shipping is not charged retroactively.

## Each item can be refunded once

An item that has already been refunded cannot be refunded again. If your order
shows the status **refunded**, the refund has already been issued. Check your
bank statement or contact support if you can't find it after 10 business days.

## Cancelled orders

Cancelled orders are never charged. The temporary authorization on your card is
released within 3–5 business days.
