# Cancelling an Order

## When you can cancel

You can cancel an order while its status is **pending**, meaning it hasn't shipped
yet. Orders placed before **1:00 pm Pacific Time** on a business day usually ship
the same day, so cancel as soon as possible.

To cancel, go to **Orders** and select **Cancel order**, or contact support with
your order number and email address.

## After an order ships

Once the status is **shipped**, we can't cancel it. You can:

- Return the items after delivery under our *Return Policy*, or
- Refuse the delivery. Once the package returns to our warehouse, we refund it
  minus the $7.95 return shipping fee.

## What happens when you cancel

Your card is never charged for a cancelled order. The temporary authorization is
released within **3–5 business days**, depending on your bank.

## Cancelling part of an order

You can remove individual items from a pending order. The rest of the order ships
as normal.

## Orders cancelled by us

Occasionally we cancel an order, for example if an item is out of stock or payment
can't be verified. We'll email you the reason, and you won't be charged.
