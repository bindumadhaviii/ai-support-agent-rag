# Price Adjustments

If the price of an item you bought drops on our website within **14 days of your
order date**, we'll refund the difference once per item.

## Conditions

- The lower price must be on the Ridgeline Outfitters website. We don't match
  other retailers' prices.
- The item must be the same product, size, and color.
- Only one price adjustment per item.
- Final sale items are not eligible.
- Discounts from promo codes, Ridgeline Rewards, or gift card promotions don't
  count as price drops.

## How to request one

Contact support with your order number and the item. The difference is refunded
to your original payment method within 2 business days.

Note that the 14-day price adjustment period starts on the **order date**, while
the 30-day return window starts on the **delivery date**.
