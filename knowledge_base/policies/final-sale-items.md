# Final Sale Items

Final sale items cannot be returned or exchanged. These include:

- **Gift cards**
- Items marked **Final Sale** on the product page, usually clearance items
  discounted **50% or more**
- **Opened** packs of Merino Hiking Socks

Final sale items are still covered by the Ridgeline Guarantee if they have a
manufacturing defect, and you can still report items that arrived damaged.

Final sale items are also not eligible for price adjustments.
