# Return Policy

We want you to love your gear. If something isn't right, you can return it within
**30 days of the delivery date** shown in your order tracking.

## What can be returned

Items can be returned if they are:

- Unused and unworn outdoors (trying on indoors is fine)
- Clean, with original tags attached
- Complete, with all parts, accessories, and packaging

Some products have extra conditions:

- **Tents** can be returned if they were only set up indoors to check them, are
  clean and dry, and include every pole, stake, guyline, and the rainfly.
- **Merino Hiking Socks** can only be returned **unopened** in their original
  packaging, for hygiene reasons.
- **Final sale items** cannot be returned. See *Final Sale Items*.

Items used outdoors are not returnable, but they may be covered by our warranty if
they have a manufacturing defect. See *Ridgeline Guarantee (Warranty)*.

## How to start a return

1. Go to **Orders** in your account, or contact support with your order number
   and email address.
2. Select the items you want to return and the reason.
3. Print the prepaid return label we email you and drop the package at any
   carrier location.

Orders placed as a guest can be returned by contacting support.

## Return shipping cost

A **$7.95 return shipping fee** is deducted from your refund. The fee is waived when:

- The item arrived damaged or defective
- We sent the wrong item
- You are a **Summit** member of Ridgeline Rewards

## After we receive your return

Returns are inspected within 2 business days of arriving at our warehouse. Once
approved, your refund is issued as described in our *Refund Policy*.

## Returns after 30 days

We cannot accept returns more than 30 days after delivery. If an item has a
defect, it may still be covered by the Ridgeline Guarantee.
