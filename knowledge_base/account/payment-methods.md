# Payment Methods

## Accepted payment methods

- Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover
- PayPal
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- Ridgeline gift cards

We don't accept cash on delivery, checks, or cryptocurrency. All prices are in
US dollars.

## When you're charged

When you place an order, we place a temporary **authorization** on your card to
confirm the funds are available. You're **charged when the order ships**. If an
order ships in several packages, you may see a separate charge for each one.

If the order is cancelled before shipping, the authorization is released within
3–5 business days and you're not charged.

## Splitting a payment

You can combine one or more gift cards with one other payment method. You can't
split an order across two credit cards.

## Sales tax

Sales tax is added at checkout for addresses in states where we're required to
collect it. International orders may owe duties and VAT on delivery.

## Payment declined

Check that the billing address matches the one your bank has on file, and that the
card hasn't expired. If it's still declined, contact your bank or try another
payment method. Our support team can't see why a bank declined a payment.
