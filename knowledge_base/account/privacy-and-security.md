# Privacy and Security

## What we will never ask for

Ridgeline Outfitters, including our support team and our AI support assistant, will
**never** ask you for:

- Your password
- Your full card number or security code (CVV)
- One-time verification codes sent to your phone or email
- Remote access to your computer

If anyone contacting you as Ridgeline asks for these, don't share them, and report
it to security@ridgeline.example.

## How we verify you

To discuss an order, support may ask for your **order number** and the **email
address** on the order. For changes like a new shipping address, we may send a
confirmation to that email address.

We can only share order details with the customer who placed the order.

## How we use your data

We use your information to process orders, provide support, and, if you opt in,
send marketing emails. We **don't sell** your personal data. Support conversations
may be reviewed to improve service quality.

## Your choices

- Unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link at the bottom of any email.
- Request a copy of your data, or ask us to delete your account and personal data,
  by emailing privacy@ridgeline.example. We respond within **30 days**.

Some order records must be kept for tax and legal reasons even after an account is
deleted.
