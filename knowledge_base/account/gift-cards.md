# Gift Cards

## Buying a gift card

Ridgeline digital gift cards are available in amounts from **$25 to $500**. They're
emailed to the recipient on the date you choose, with your personal message.

## Using a gift card

Enter the 16-digit code at checkout. Any remaining balance stays on the card for
next time. You can combine gift cards with one other payment method.

## Terms

- Gift cards **never expire** and have no fees.
- They can't be exchanged for cash, except where required by law.
- Gift cards are **final sale** and can't be returned or refunded.
- Refunds for items paid with a gift card go back to the gift card.

## Checking your balance

Use **Gift cards → Check balance** at the bottom of any page.

## Lost gift cards

If the purchaser can give us the gift card order number, we can resend the email
to the recipient. We can't replace a gift card that has already been used by
someone else.
