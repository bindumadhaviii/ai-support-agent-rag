# Your Account and Signing In

## Do I need an account?

No. You can check out as a guest. With an account you can track orders, start
returns and exchanges yourself, save addresses, and earn Ridgeline Rewards points.

## Creating an account

Select **Sign up** at the top of any page, or tick **Create an account** at
checkout. If you already ordered as a guest with the same email address, those
orders are added to your new account.

## Forgot your password

1. Select **Sign in → Forgot password**.
2. Enter your account email address.
3. Use the link we email you. It **expires after 30 minutes**.

Our support team can't see or reset your password for you, and will never ask you
for it.

## Changing your email address

Go to **Account → Settings → Email**. We send a confirmation link to the new
address, and the change takes effect once you click it.

## Looking up an order without signing in

Use **Track an order** at the bottom of any page and enter your order number and
the email address used for the order.

## Closing your account

Contact support or see *Privacy and Security* to request account deletion.
