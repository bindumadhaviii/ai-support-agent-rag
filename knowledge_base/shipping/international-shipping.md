# International Shipping

We ship to **Canada**, the **United Kingdom**, and all **European Union** countries.

## Rates and delivery times

International orders ship at a flat rate of **$24.95** and arrive in
**7–14 business days**. Free shipping on orders of $75 or more applies to US
orders only.

## Duties and taxes

Prices on our website don't include import duties, VAT, or customs fees. These are
charged by your country's customs authority and **paid by you on delivery**. We
can't estimate them in advance or refund them.

If you refuse a package because of duties, it's returned to us and refunded minus
the original shipping charge and any return costs we're charged.

## International returns

The same 30-day return window applies. International customers pay for return
shipping and should use a tracked service. The $7.95 prepaid return label is
available for US addresses only.

## Not available internationally

- Expedited and Overnight shipping
- Basecamp Rechargeable Lantern to some EU countries, due to battery shipping rules.
  The product page shows whether it can ship to your address.
