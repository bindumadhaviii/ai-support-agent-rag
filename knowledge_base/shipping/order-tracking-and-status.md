# Order Tracking and Status

## Finding your tracking number

When your order ships, we email you a tracking number. Ridgeline tracking numbers
start with **TRK** followed by six digits, for example TRK123456. You can also find
it under **Orders** in your account.

Tracking information can take up to **24 hours** to appear after you receive the
shipping email.

## What each order status means

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending | We've received your order and it hasn't shipped yet. You can still cancel it or change the address. |
| Shipped | The order has left our warehouse and is on its way. It can no longer be cancelled. |
| Delivered | The carrier has marked the order as delivered. The 30-day return window starts on this date. |
| Cancelled | The order was cancelled before shipping. You were not charged. |
| Refunded | A refund has been issued for the order. |

## Orders with several packages

Larger orders may ship in more than one box, each with its own tracking number.
Each package is listed separately under **Orders**.

## Tracking hasn't updated

It's normal for tracking to pause for a day or two in transit. If there's been no
update for **5 business days**, contact us and we'll look into it. See
*Lost, Stolen, or Delayed Packages*.
