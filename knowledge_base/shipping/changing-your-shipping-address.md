# Changing Your Shipping Address

## Before your order ships

You can change the shipping address while your order status is **pending**. Go to
**Orders**, select the order, and choose **Edit address**, or contact support.

For your security, we may ask you to confirm the email address on the order before
changing the address.

## After your order ships

Once an order has shipped, we can't change the address. You may be able to
redirect the package through the carrier's website using your tracking number.
Redirect options and fees depend on the carrier.

## Changing your saved address

To update the default address for future orders, go to **Account → Addresses**.
This doesn't change orders you've already placed.
