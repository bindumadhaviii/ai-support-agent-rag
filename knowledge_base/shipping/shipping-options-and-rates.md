# Shipping Options and Rates

We ship to all 50 US states, Canada, the United Kingdom, and the European Union.

## Contiguous United States

| Method | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 5–7 business days | $6.95, **free on orders of $75 or more** |
| Expedited | 2–3 business days | $14.95 |
| Overnight | 1 business day | $29.95 |

The free shipping threshold is based on your order total after discounts and before
tax. Summit members of Ridgeline Rewards get free Expedited shipping.

## Alaska, Hawaii, and US territories

Only Standard shipping is available, taking **7–10 business days** for **$12.95**.
Free shipping on orders of $75 or more does not apply.

## Processing times

Orders placed before **1:00 pm Pacific Time**, Monday to Friday, usually ship the
same day. Orders placed later, or on weekends and US holidays, ship the next
business day. Delivery times start from the ship date, not the order date.

## Restrictions

- Overnight shipping is not available to PO boxes or APO/FPO addresses.
- PO boxes can receive Standard shipping only.

For shipping outside the US, see *International Shipping*.
