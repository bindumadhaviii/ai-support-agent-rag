# Lost, Stolen, or Delayed Packages

## Marked delivered but not received

Packages sometimes show as delivered slightly before they arrive. Before
contacting us:

1. Wait **48 hours** after the delivered scan.
2. Check around your property, mailroom, and with neighbors.
3. Confirm the shipping address on your order.

If you still don't have it, contact support within **14 days** of the delivered
date. We'll open an investigation with the carrier, which takes up to
**5 business days**. Once it's complete, we'll send a replacement or issue a refund.

## Delayed packages

If tracking hasn't updated for **5 business days**, contact us with your order
number. We'll check with the carrier. If the carrier confirms the package is lost,
we'll ship a replacement right away or refund you, whichever you prefer.

## Returned to sender

If a package is returned to us because the address was wrong or incomplete, we'll
contact you. We can reship it once you confirm the address. You pay the shipping
cost again if the address error was on the order.
