# Alpine Insulated Jacket (JKT-001)

**Price:** $189.00

A warm, lightweight synthetic jacket for cold hikes, camp evenings, and winter
commutes. Synthetic insulation stays warm even when damp, unlike down.

## Specifications

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Insulation | 100 g/m² synthetic fill |
| Shell | Recycled ripstop nylon with water-repellent (DWR) finish |
| Weight | 540 g (men's medium) |
| Comfort range | Down to about -10°C / 14°F while active |
| Pockets | 2 zippered hand pockets, 1 internal chest pocket |
| Packs into | Its own internal chest pocket |
| Fit | Regular fit, room for a midlayer underneath |

The jacket is water-resistant, not waterproof. In heavy rain, wear a rain shell over it.

## Care

- Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle with a mild detergent.
- Do not use fabric softener or bleach.
- Tumble dry on low heat until fully dry.
- Do not dry clean or iron.
- If water stops beading on the surface, refresh the DWR finish with a wash-in or
  spray-on treatment.

## Sizing

See *Size Charts* for chest measurements. If you're between sizes and plan to
layer underneath, choose the larger size.
