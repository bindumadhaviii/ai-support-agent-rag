# Trailhead Waterproof Boots (BOOT-002)

**Price:** $149.00

Mid-height hiking boots for day hikes and light backpacking, with a waterproof,
breathable membrane.

## Specifications

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Upper | Suede leather and nylon mesh |
| Waterproofing | Waterproof, breathable membrane |
| Outsole | Rubber with 5 mm lugs |
| Midsole | Cushioned EVA foam |
| Weight | 1.1 kg per pair (men's US 9) |
| Height | Mid (above the ankle) |

## Fit

These boots **run about half a size small**. If you're between sizes, or plan to
wear thick hiking socks, order **half a size up**. See *Size Charts* for US, UK,
and EU conversions.

Allow **15–20 miles (25–30 km)** of walking to break them in before a long hike.

## Care

- Brush off mud and dirt after each use.
- Rinse with clean water. Don't use detergent or soap on the leather.
- Air dry at room temperature, away from heaters, fires, and direct sun.
- Never put boots in a washing machine or dryer. Heat damages the membrane and glue.
- Treat the leather with a waterproofing product made for suede every few months.

## Warranty

membrane. Worn-down outsoles are normal wear and not covered.
