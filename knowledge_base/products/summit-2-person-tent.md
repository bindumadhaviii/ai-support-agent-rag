# Summit 2-Person Tent (TENT-003)

**Price:** $329.00

A freestanding, three-season backpacking tent for two people, with two doors and
two vestibules.

## Specifications

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|

| Seasons | 3-season (spring, summer, fall) |
| Trail weight | 1.9 kg (tent body, rainfly, poles) |
| Packed weight | 2.3 kg (everything included) |
| Floor size | 218 × 132 cm |
| Peak height | 104 cm |
| Doors / vestibules | 2 / 2 |
| Poles | Aluminum, color-coded |
| Setup time | About 5 minutes once practiced |

## What's in the bag

Tent body, rainfly, poles, 10 aluminum stakes, 4 guylines, a pole repair sleeve,
and a stuff sack. A footprint (ground sheet) is not included.

## Setup tips

1. Lay the tent body flat with the doors where you want them.
2. Connect the color-coded poles and clip the tent body to them.
3. Drape the rainfly over the top, matching the colored corners, and buckle it down.
4. Stake out the corners and vestibules, then the guylines in wind.

Practice setting it up at home first. Indoor setup does not affect returns.

## Care

- Never pack the tent away wet for more than **24 hours**. Mildew damage isn't
  covered by the warranty.
- Dry it fully before storing, loosely in a large breathable bag rather than the
  stuff sack.
- Clean by hand with a sponge and cold water. Never machine wash or dry.
- Use the pole repair sleeve to splint a bent or broken pole in the field.

## Returns

Tents can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they were only set up indoors,
are clean and dry, and include every part.
