## Finding your fit

1. Measure your **torso length**: from the bony bump at the base of your neck (C7
   vertebra) down to the line between the tops of your hip bones.
2. Loosen all straps, then slide the shoulder harness to your torso length using
   the ladder on the back panel.
3. Load the pack with about 7 kg and put it on.
4. Tighten the **hipbelt** first, centered on your hip bones. Most of the weight
   should sit on your hips.
5. Tighten the shoulder straps, then the load lifters, then the sternum strap.

If your torso is shorter than 40 cm or longer than 51 cm, this pack may not fit
comfortably.

## Care

Empty the pack and shake out debris after each trip. Hand wash with mild soap and
cold water, then hang to dry out of direct sun. Do not machine wash.
