# Basecamp Rechargeable Lantern (LAMP-006)

**Price:** $45.00

A rechargeable camp lantern with four light modes that can also charge your phone.

## Specifications

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Max brightness | 400 lumens |
| Battery | 5,200 mAh lithium-ion (built in) |
| Charging port | USB-C |
| Power bank output | USB-A, to charge a phone |
| Water resistance | IPX4: splash-resistant, not waterproof |
| Weight | 310 g |

## Light modes and runtime

| Mode | Brightness | Runtime on a full charge |
|---|---|---|
| Low | 50 lumens | About 40 hours |
| Medium | 150 lumens | About 14 hours |
| High | 400 lumens | About 5 hours |
| Red | Red light (protects night vision) | About 60 hours |

Press the power button to turn on, press again to change modes, and hold for
2 seconds to turn off.

## Charging

- A full charge takes about **4 hours** with a 2 A USB charger.
- The four battery lights show the charge level and blink while charging.
- Using it as a power bank reduces the lantern's runtime.

## Battery care

- The battery is rated for about 500 full charge cycles and is not user-replaceable.
- For long-term storage, leave it at about half charge and top it up every
  3 months.
- Don't leave it in a hot car or submerge it in water.

## Troubleshooting

If the lantern won't turn on, charge it for at least 30 minutes with a different
USB-C cable and charger. If it still doesn't work, contact support. It may be
covered by the warranty.

## Warranty

Covered by the Ridgeline Guarantee for **1 year**, including the battery.
