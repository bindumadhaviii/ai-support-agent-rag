# Contact Us

## Chat

Our AI support assistant is available in chat **24/7**. It can answer questions,
look up your orders, and start returns and refunds.

You can ask to speak with a person at any time. During support hours, the assistant
hands your conversation to a team member with a summary, so you don't have to
repeat yourself. Outside support hours, it creates a ticket and a team member
replies by email on the next business day.

## Human support hours (Pacific Time)

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday – Friday | 6:00 am – 6:00 pm |
| Saturday | 8:00 am – 2:00 pm |
| Sunday and US holidays | Closed |

## Before you contact us

Have your **order number** and the **email address** on the order ready. For
damaged items or warranty claims, include photos.
