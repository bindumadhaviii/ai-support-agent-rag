# Ridgeline Rewards

Ridgeline Rewards is our free loyalty program. Join by creating an account.

## Earning points

- Earn **1 point for every $1** spent on products (not shipping, tax, or gift cards).
- Points are added **after the 30-day return window closes** for the order.
- If you return an item, the points for it are removed.

## Using points

Every **100 points** becomes a **$5 reward**, applied automatically to your account.
Rewards can be used at checkout and **expire 90 days** after they're issued.

## Tiers

| Tier | Points earned in a calendar year | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Trail | 0–499 | Points and rewards, birthday offer |
| Summit | 500 or more | Everything in Trail, plus free Expedited shipping and free return shipping |

Summit status lasts for the rest of the year you qualify and the whole following
year.

## Points and refunds

Rewards used on an order aren't refunded as cash. If you return items bought with a
reward, the reward amount is reissued to your account with a new 90-day expiry.
