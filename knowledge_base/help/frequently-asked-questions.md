# Frequently Asked Questions

## Orders

**Where is my order?**
Check **Orders** in your account or use the tracking number in your shipping email.
See *Order Tracking and Status* for what each status means.

**Can I cancel my order?**
Yes, while its status is pending. Once it has shipped, you'll need to return it after
delivery. See *Cancelling an Order*.

**Can I add an item to an order I already placed?**
No. Place a new order instead. If both orders are pending, contact support and we'll
try to ship them together.

## Returns and refunds

**How long do I have to return something?**
30 days from the delivery date.

**Is return shipping free?**
A $7.95 fee is deducted from your refund, unless the item was damaged, defective, or
wrong, or you're a Summit rewards member.

**When will I get my refund?**
We issue it within 2 business days of receiving your return. Your bank usually shows
it within 5–10 business days. Refunds over $200 are reviewed by our team first,
which adds up to 1 business day.

**My item broke after a few months. Can I return it?**
Returns are only accepted within 30 days, but manufacturing defects are covered by
the Ridgeline Guarantee for 1 or 2 years. See *Ridgeline Guarantee (Warranty)*.

## Products

**Do your boots run true to size?**
They run about half a size small. Order half a size up if you're between sizes.

**Is the Alpine Insulated Jacket waterproof?**
No. It's water-resistant. Wear a rain shell over it in heavy rain.

**Does the tent come with a footprint?**
No, a footprint isn't included.

## Other

**Do you have physical stores?**
No. Ridgeline Outfitters is online only.

**Do you offer student or military discounts?**
Not at this time. Join Ridgeline Rewards to earn rewards on every order.

**Can I talk to a real person?**
Yes. Ask the chat assistant at any time, or see *Contact Us* for hours, email, and
phone.
